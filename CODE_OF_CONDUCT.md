# Code of conduct

Treat other contributors with respect. Disagree with ideas and code without attacking the people behind them. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing someone else's private information without consent are not acceptable.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, and other spaces maintained by the project. Report concerns privately to a maintainer; do not post personal details in a public issue. For abuse on GitHub, you can [report it to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies#4-reporting-abuse).

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with actions proportionate to the conduct.
